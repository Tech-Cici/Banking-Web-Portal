========================= WHY A TRANSFER IS REFUSED =============================

Sending money checks three things about the FROM account, and refuses with
"We could not find the account you are sending from." if any of them fails.
This prints all three, per account, so a refusal names a field instead of a guess.

  owner        the row must belong to the signed-in customer
  company_id   must be EMPTY for a personal account. A company account is reached
               through a LIVE membership and never through this column
  removed_at   must be empty. A closed account cannot be debited

HOW TO READ IT. The "From" menu offers exactly the accounts that say CAN SEND, so
if the account you chose says CAN SEND here and the portal still refuses it, the
account is fine and the id the form sent is not one of these — look at the
sourceAccountId in the browser Network tab and compare it to the column below.
